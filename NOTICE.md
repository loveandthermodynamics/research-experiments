# Provenance and reuse

This repository is a curated research snapshot prepared at Simon Hill's request. It preserves exploratory scripts, declared models, compact results and evidence of failed expectations. Its publication does not establish that a model describes living organisms, validates Hestia, or contributes a novel result to the field.

No new software or data license is selected by this release. Existing notices and attribution remain applicable to the files that carry them. The Hestia specification's CC BY-NC 4.0 notice does not automatically license every implementation, independent research script or dataset. Arrange access, reuse and contribution terms with the maintainer before substantial derivative or collaborative work; see the [program contribution guide](https://github.com/loveandthermodynamics/research-program/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md).

The [Love & Thermodynamics reuse policy](https://loveandthermodynamics.com/reuse) explains the terms for explicitly marked project writing and how to request commercial permission. For material in this archive, check the notices for the specific files you want to use.

Selected research source files are copied byte-for-byte wherever the provenance manifest says so. Newly written documentation, extraction of synthetic aggregate results and any publication-only metadata/path adaptations are identified separately. Dates and saved outcomes describe the historical record; a later reproduction is reported as a separate event.

Third-party papers, implementations, raw conversations, participant records, provider traces and credentials are not part of this release. References acknowledge the published foundations used by the research; they are not a claim to redistribute those sources or to have independently validated their conclusions.
