# Coverage and provenance

The evidence discovery described here is dated November 7. The November 8 editorial revision adds [an interpretation guide](interpreting-results.md), clearer current study entries and context for historical terminology. It adds no model outcome, efficacy result or source-discovery claim. All 48 dated physical Markdown notes, scientific scripts, protocols and result files retain their previous publication bytes.

This archive assembles the recovered, topic-relevant research located by 7 November 2026. It brings together early conditional calculations, physical model studies, theoretical and thought experiments, and Hestia implementation checks. The aim is to account for the full recovered indexed work without treating every mention, intermediate copy or proposed experiment as a separate completed result.

It is not a claim to have recovered every experiment ever performed. Source histories begin at different dates, some indexed lanes stop earlier than others, and current work can extend beyond the latest indexed event.

## How the sources were located

Discovery began with a maintained registry of ten activity sources. Searches covered their full indexed histories, with no date filter, using Hestia, individuation, physical diversity, regeneration, negentropy, least action and fat-tail terms. The primary query returned all 401 matches; a second targeted query covered Lagrangian, speciation, organizational closure, response diversity and related phrases. No index was missing or unreadable, and no index refresh was run during discovery.

Index matches were used as leads into primary artifacts: editable research documents, model code, protocols, saved outputs, verification records and relevant raw tool histories. Related drafts and copies were reconciled by content, provenance and hashes. Index counts are discovery counts, not numbers of experiments.

The source corpus spans Hestia's implementation and research material, the author's essay and editorial research corpus, and the historical tool records linking their development. Unrelated personal, clinical and project material was excluded. Private case text, contact details and raw activity histories are not part of the public coverage report.

The early model families include regeneration and patient-predation comparisons; response diversity with correlation and maintenance costs; exact prediction-versus-control policies; conditional channel value and refusal; a solved variational controller and scaling counterexample; multiplicative persistence and fat-tail constructions; and reward-free chemical assembly with niche succession.

The topic digests and register preserve both positive findings and failed or inconclusive prospective claims.

The software evidence distinguishes an October 22 offline audit from a November 7 replay of three characterization probes. Replaying those probes does not rerun the earlier 255-test suite, execute a population study or establish moral development. Fictional adversarial cases and proposed care, independence and collective-agency studies are labeled separately from executed code.

Article publication dates identify conceptual predecessors. A later export does not prove that every current paragraph existed on the original publication date. Later argument repairs are retained as such rather than silently presented as findings already established by the earlier essays.

## Copies, recovery and reproducibility

An early chemical prototype was retained in its original folder and copied into the common physical research corpus. Its source and result hashes match; the archive treats it as one study. Temporary paths that later vanished do not imply lost research when matching persistent copies survive. The recovered offline audit scripts are an example.

Public source and aggregate evidence are distinguished from complete raw histories. The large physical phases have stored independent replay and verification records; a compact public package may omit their full raw trajectory files. Such an omission limits independent event-by-event reproduction from the public package alone and is stated with the relevant study. Stored verification is not a claim that a fresh simulation was rerun while preparing this archive.

Some public diagnostic scripts require separately agreed access to a matching Hestia implementation. Source revision and evidence hashes establish what was inspected; they do not make private runtime code available. A new seed also cannot reproduce live model responses unless those responses and the other relevant initial conditions are preserved.

A study record should therefore identify its actual status: proposed design, conditional proof, executed model, implementation probe, replay, review or human-participant result. Mathematical checking, software correctness, field-level novelty, participant benefit and subjective experience remain separate claims.

## Continuing the record

New evidence should extend a named study or add a clearly bounded one, preserving its original protocol, revisions and outcomes. A newly recovered source should be added with its provenance and the change it makes to coverage. Until then, uncovered history remains unknown rather than being recorded as zero work.
