# Collective agency and care: testing what the whole makes possible

Within Love & Thermodynamics, care means helping lives and possibilities continue, including when someone cannot repay that care. The collective question is whether cooperation can create an organization with new capacities and an identity of its own, while widening its participants' possibilities for development. Their development might then enable further relationships and organization. Members and wholes can change through that process; responsibility for care, burdens and objections must remain consequential.

Our Group Mind proposal extends prediction and correction to the collective level. A group commits to an expectation about members' evaluations, acts, receives their answers, revises its account of them and of itself, and changes a subsequent decision. Its history contains commitments, mistakes and repairs for which others can hold it answerable.

**COSSHAIC** is the constitutional proposal connecting that collective cognition to legitimate, revisable authority and material support. Its [founding account](https://medium.com/love-and-thermodynamics/a-constitutional-operating-system-for-a-symbiotic-human-ai-civilization-48b1a99bf826) and [appendix](https://metalaureate.medium.com/appendix-to-a-constitutional-operating-system-for-a-symbiotic-human-ai-civilization-116d158ecedc) supply these commitments; the [study guide](https://github.com/loveandthermodynamics/research-program/blob/main/docs/cosshaic.md) connects them to open work. This names the architecture already behind the case, rather than declaring a new completed system.

This digest brings together completed conceptual checks and the proposed care study. No human-benefit or collective-AI efficacy experiment has been completed in this evidence set.

**The care case**

In the [published invitation](https://medium.com/p/144488662910), Leila misses repair shifts while caring for Sam, who is ill. Jo covers the work. The cooperative's AI records Leila's absence and recommends an extra obligation, while treating the repairs as successfully completed.

A correction must do more than relabel Leila as caring. Jo needs relief from the accumulated burden. Sam needs continuity of care, including when unable to explain a need or reciprocate. The facts needed to correct an allocation should not become general institutional knowledge merely because they are relevant to it.

The positive possibility is a continuing arrangement none could sustain alone: Leila can undertake responsibilities previously beyond reach, Jo can set limits without precipitating a crisis, and care continues through personnel changes. Those capacities might support further commitments. Establishing that sequence requires more than a fairer score or a satisfied group.

**What the completed checks contribute**

An early finite model enumerated eight policies with independently specified needs. A controller could predict public reports perfectly while meeting only half the needs. A policy with the same protected prediction accuracy as a responsive learner could deliberately reverse the need and meet none.

That result is limited but useful: prediction, responsiveness and welfare require separate measurements. A causal response can have the wrong direction. In the toy, both allocations have equal costs and no conflicting claims; that simplification cannot resolve a real cooperative's contested obligations. See the [model and declared assumptions](../../experiments/candidate-model/README.md).

The same early work solved a reduced resource-allocation controller for two maintained capacities and a resource stock. Within supplied dynamics, constraints and objective, an analytical loss identity identifies a global optimum; numerical checks agree. This establishes that an explicitly chosen allocation problem can be mathematically coherent. The objective and capacity floors are modeling choices, not ethical conclusions derived from physics. The variables are not calibrated persons, and the controller is not an implemented Group Mind.

The software audit supplies another caution. Explicit feedback can alter a message's recorded evaluation without revising the learned state. That [implementation failure](../../experiments/software-correctness/README.md) was checked through the Django view and database, without a browser. It is not an institutional study, but it shows why correction must be followed through dependent state and action rather than counted when an API accepts it.

**The proposed comparison**

Before the comparison, declare the cooperative's care rule, mandate and authority to act. Sam can authorize a bounded witness examination without authorizing general disclosure. Leila and Jo need a challenge route independent of the controller of the disputed account. The warranted correction must reach an authorized change to Leila's obligation, relief for Jo and continuity for Sam, with the cost of repair counted. The experiment must identify who may amend the rule or revoke representation. A recommendation, signed attestation or saved objection is not that whole chain.

A bounded care-and-work environment would compare three arrangements:

1. Ordinary coordination with an adequate record of the situation and the same constitutional protections.
2. Coordination with a contestable account, consequential corrections and stated constitutional protections.
3. The same arrangement with Hestia's additional prediction, self-model and feeling mechanisms.

Information, authority, action opportunities, resource ceilings and constitutional protections must match. An ordinary coordinator should not be deliberately deprived of context to make Hestia appear helpful. The comparison should distinguish the value of a contestable account from any additional value of agent cognition.

Outcomes include care delivered, unequal burden, correction effort, privacy exposure, refusal consequences and newly enabled capabilities. An independent scorer must inspect actual allocations and changes. Increased engagement, reputation or internal fitness cannot replace those outcomes.

Before running the comparison, specify what people and the collective could undertake before and after the proposed development. Track those possibilities through changes of membership, role or identity, alongside continuing obligations to dependent people. A matching ordinary coordinator, absent new capacities, or gains bought by shifting burdens onto others must count against the specified developmental claim. The claim cannot be rescued simply by renaming coordination as an emergent identity.

A warranted correction should propagate into obligations that depended on the mistake. Some cost of creating or preserving an incorrect attribution should return to the responsible part of the collective; Jo should not repeatedly pay to establish the same burden. Records need purposes and expiry. An accurate fact can still be harmful when retained or disclosed outside its justified use.

**The recursive question**

A cooperative may join an association and make promises of its own. The parent needs an authorized account of those commitments, with relevant challenges and qualifications intact. It need not inherit unrestricted access to each member's interior. Fair Witness names the proposed responsibility for that account, rather than an automatic guarantee of truth.

We need to test whether an affected outsider's objection survives composition, whether a correction changes the parent's decision, and what happens when membership, authority or personnel changes. Constituting a group by assigning it a label would not demonstrate that a new individual had formed.

COSSHAIC also asks whether experience changes the collective's purposes and what it can promise. Trace the authority for a revised commitment, the qualifications sent upward and the resources that make continuing care possible. Group and member identities may develop together; the proposed test concerns that new capacity and continuing answerability, not merely administration of an unchanged roster.

A useful contribution is one failure remaining after an apparently successful correction, together with a specific relief or new capacity that would count as improvement. Another is a minimal scenario and independent scorer for the three-arm comparison. These are proposed experiments. Human participation would require a separate agreed protocol for consent, data and review after the simulated mechanics are reliable.
