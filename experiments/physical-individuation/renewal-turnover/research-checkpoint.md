# Renewed constraints: a proved mechanism and an unresolved spatial test

The full biodiversity, individuation and Unitology objective remains unproved.
This checkpoint adds an audited negative spatial result, an exact conditional
maintenance threshold, and a counterexample that rules out an entropy-only
shortcut to the proposed variational explanation. These are different findings
in different models; the mathematical construction does not turn the failed
spatial assay into a successful one.

## The finite-budget spatial test

The previous lattice supplied local reversible copying, production of L and
L-dependent protection. A new bounded pilot increased symmetric passive L
turnover to .08, .16 and .32 during both formation and continuation. All other
physical parameters were fixed. Eight fresh preparations per level were grown
from dispersed templates with zero L for time 20 and branched into four
production-by-protection arms for time 80. No preparation was filtered.

The frozen pilot rule selected .16. A fresh 64-preparation confirmation used
the same design and retained one extinct preparation in all four arms.

| Primary paired contrast | Mean R-time effect | Approximate 95 percent interval |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Continuing L production when protection works | 4.56 | -15.55 to 24.66 |
| Production-by-protection interaction | 34.32 | 10.47 to 58.17 |

The declared joint claim required both intervals to lie above zero and failed.
The interaction alone would be misleading: production without protection
reduces activity by 29.77, while its net protected benefit is uncertain.
Legacy protection still has a positive continuation benefit. L is actually
renewed, chiefly by paid production, but its functional necessity is not
established by this finite-horizon assay. No further turnover tuning was done.

Independent replay covered 88 preparations and 352 continuations: 440 distinct
records, 310,915 physical events and 15,400 samples. Preparation is counted
once rather than once per branch. All seed cells, physical branch states,
formation costs, source/protocol hashes, pilot selection and paired statistics
passed. The extinct-preparation episode readout is corrected only in the new
runner; its integrated-activity readout was unaffected. Existing model and
evidence files were preserved.

The finite closed lattice has an initial availability budget. Detailed balance
gives integral sigma dt = D(p0||pi)-D(pT||pi), bounded by its starting relative
entropy. It therefore tests finite-horizon persistence, not a stationary
through-flow organization. See `finite-budget-bound.md`. Integrated template
occupancy is not itself thermodynamic negentropy.

## An exact reciprocal-maintenance threshold

The separate open construction has precursor D, template R and protective L,
with fixed total material D+R+L=M. Copying and protective production consume
fuel; decay returns material to D. Continuing fuel supply and waste removal
are explicit chemostats with counted chemical work. Copying, catalysis and
the protection law are supplied physical premises, not newly derived chemistry.

In the negligible-reverse approximation,

    dR/dt = R[a(M-R-L) - delta exp(-alpha L)]
    dL/dt = bR(M-R-L) - gamma L.

Set c=delta/a and beta=b/gamma. Positive stationary states are exactly roots of

    M = F(L) = L + c exp(-alpha L) + L exp(alpha L)/(beta c).

F is strictly convex for alpha>0; take its minimum over L>=0. In the regime M<c, unprotected templates
cannot sustain themselves. A stable positive state and a separating saddle
exist exactly when min F<M<c. A nonempty interval of suitable inventories
exists exactly when

    alpha c > 1 + 1/(beta c).

This is a dynamical saddle-node threshold. It is not a demonstrated singular
thermodynamic phase transition or a universal law. It arises from local rate
feedback and material balance without a group or diversity reward.

A consistent finite-temperature completion retains every reverse channel.
For a=b=delta=gamma=1, alpha=4, M=.95, internal energies 20, fuel affinity 40
and theta=1, all omitted reverse coefficients are eta=exp(-20). Outward
interval arithmetic certifies a low stable state, a saddle and a high stable
state with R between .232847617045 and .232847617048. It certifies one unique
root in each bracket and their stability, without asserting global root
exhaustiveness. Analytical ablation bounds place stationary R below 3.92e-8
if either protection or paid L production is removed. Matched intervention
trajectories independently confirm loss of the high state.

At the high state, active templates and protection are continually replaced.
The chemical power is 10.835871084 normalized energy per volume-time and equals
theta times entropy production at stationarity. The full free-energy balance
also passes away from stationarity. The high state is maintained by work;
greater dissipation is not presented as moral value or a minimum-cost proof.
The theorem, physical completion and assumptions are in `theory-bridge.md`;
numerical and interval certificates are retained separately.

## The variational route must retain kinetics

For a jump history, log probability includes log transition rates and the
integral of escape rates. A fluctuation theorem constrains a history relative
to its reverse; it does not rank arbitrary forward histories by dissipation.
The exact five-state counterexample has two positive-entropy paths: 2 kB and
1 kB. The higher-entropy path has only .005084 times the density and .006232
times the finite-event probability. Both forward/reverse fluctuation ratios
still hold. Symmetric kinetic barriers supply the difference.

This blocks an entropy-only forward-history ranking, including the explicit
ansatz in [Segal's 2026 preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.15230).
The full path action retains a legitimate conditional variational formulation,
including the dynamical activity emphasized by
[Maes](https://arxiv.org/abs/1705.07412). It still needs a physical generator
that produces the proposed organizations. See `path-ranking-audit.md` and its
executable counterexample.

The current exported Genesis text values sustained differentiated metabolic
pathways. That continuity matters: participant flourishing is not an arbitrary
new patch. But the exported Lagrangian and Evil appendices make stronger
identifications between prediction error, physical entropy, trajectory action
and inevitable moral selection than these results establish. In particular,
integrating a quantity over time does not make it Hamiltonian action, and a
durable coercive collective remains a physical possibility.

A valid Unitology defense retains the independently consequential interests
and development of participants as an ethical premise. Conditional physical
results can show when integration sustains those capacities. They cannot
make their value disappear whenever a more efficient whole could replace
them. Voluntary fusion, development and sacrifice remain available; continuing
selves need not have immutable boundaries. See the final section of
`theory-bridge.md` for the precise conditional argument.

The next physical connection is an explicit driven spatial model in which
local production and transport generate a renewing organization, with tests
that distinguish its own maintenance from a common bath. Formation from zero
L and an allowed founder budget must be tested separately. Only then can
response variation be joined to this same maintenance mechanism and repeated
across scales. The full goal remains active; this checkpoint does not claim
completion.
