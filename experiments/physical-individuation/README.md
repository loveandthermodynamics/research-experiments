# How can renewal give rise to a new individual?

Life persists by renewing the conditions that make its activity possible. Within the larger [Love & Thermodynamics inquiry](https://loveandthermodynamics.com/), these models ask how productive processes might sustain one another, form an organization with capacities of its own, and make further forms of life possible.

The difficult step is formation. Keeping supplied templates active, repairing a wound, copying descendants and producing a dense cluster answer different questions. Each study declares its own criteria. None of these observations alone establishes a new individual, still less a moral entitlement to absorb its participants.

## How this bears on the larger hypothesis

These failures constrain the tested mechanisms. A failed formation criterion cannot be replaced after the result by a favorable renewal count; an inconclusive benefit does not establish its absence. Exact counterexamples can reject a broader inference within their stated model class—for example, complementary roles do not require separate taxa when a generalist or one genotype can realize them.

The proposed developmental sequence would require an organization that acquires new capacities, consequential relations among its participants, and further possibilities created by their interaction. No completed study here demonstrates that whole sequence. Human or AI care, changing identities and responsibility require additional evidence and moral argument beyond chemical performance.

## Reading the historical notes

The October notes are preserved as dated research records, including failed expectations and terms used during the work. “Unitology” is a fictional religion from *Dead Space*, used here as an adversarial image of a successful successor that absorbs people while erasing their interests and possibilities. It is not an experimentally tested doctrine. The question is what would distinguish such absorption from development that remains answerable to those involved; integration or a changed identity alone is not a failure.
