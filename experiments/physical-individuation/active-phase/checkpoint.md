## The physical question

Can a dispersed supply of soluble material produce dense, maintained spatial units from molecular interactions, without an observer supplying droplet boundaries or a preferred group size? This is a prerequisite test, not the whole definition of an individual. The mechanism is motivated by [Kirschbaum and Zwicker's primary condensate study](https://arxiv.org/abs/2103.02921); our finite lattice is a new discrete generator, not their solver or a claimed reproduction of their phase diagram. The authors' MIT source was inspected read-only at commit `912f3041fb2f8b030bc02f14a523307ca86a0aaf`.

Each lattice site contains solvent S, soluble A or sticky B, with a second mobile enzyme layer. One Hamiltonian supplies B-B attraction and enzyme-B binding. Passive A↔B and a separate enzyme-catalyzed B+fuel↔A+waste channel create an explicit driven cycle. Every enabled transition has a finite reverse and obeys local detailed balance. The ideal homogeneous fuel bath, molecules, reaction alphabet and interactions are premises. Enzymes are conserved, not copied or renewed. The natural rates never inspect clusters, group identities or morphology readouts.

For each microscopic event, heat into the bath is chemical work minus the energy change. The accepted history records all three and signed fuel conversion. Heat is not the full entropy-production functional, and neither is Hamiltonian or stochastic action. Initial molecular preparation and reservoir construction are outside this cost ledger.

## A distinct exact bridge toward productive differentiation

The proposed copying grammar has X consume A and produce B, and Y consume B and return A. Each copy consumes template precursor and fuel. Fixed copying error multiplies the paired forward and reverse channels; thermal births and all reverse reactions remain positive. The ideal homogeneous reduction has an exactly determined, locally stable two-role fixed point for the stated parameter range. In the checked example each type has concentration .941394, versus a thermal-only baseline .004933. This is local stability in an ideal vessel, not finite-population ancestry, a spatial phase or global attraction.

A separate finite-state balance law gives, when either productive reaction family is blocked in both directions,

`E[template count] <= (maximum absolute background net A/B current + maximum total non-copy template birth intensity) / minimum per-template loss rate`.

The bound includes every background conversion, including the conserved enzyme's driven channel if retained, and every thermal or external non-copy template birth. Large bypass capacity can make it vacuous. It applies at stationarity, with a separately proved finite-time stored-inventory correction. A stationary mean template population above this bound cannot be sustained after either productive-family ablation, under the stated assumptions. That does not require separate taxa: a generalist or one genotype with developmental specialization could supply both transformations. An added cheaper copying bypass can also invalidate the necessity premise.

Independent symbolic and numerical checks support this bridge:192 positive-parameter fixtures,16 count-level copying detailed-balance pairs, positive thermal/reverse rates, material balances, local eigenvalues and stationary/finite-time inequalities. Review corrected one spatial dissipation formula to integrate local reaction density over volume. That continuum balance presumes periodic/no-flux boundaries, or explicit boundary chemical power. The phase lattice is periodic. No theorem was silently transferred from the ideal vessel to the spatial model.

## Putting the argument back together

The working chain is sustained flow → reciprocal renewal → spatial integrity → useful productive differentiation → inherited regulation through damage and replacement → further individuation. Current evidence supports conditional pieces in different models, with important failed links retained. It does not prove that the entire chain follows from cosmic least action.

The next theoretical task is a joined thermodynamic model whose material supply and consumption admit dense finite structures and whose paid productive variants help maintain them. Generalist and bypass alternatives must be evaluated within that same generator. This phase failure is a constraint on the supplied regime, not permission to tune until a favored picture appears. Formation, maintenance, turnover, heredity, informative damage history and recursive differentiation must remain separate tests.

Unitology, the Borg and Resident Evil still expose a further distinction: an efficiently maintained whole can erase the capacities or interests of its constituents. The participant criterion asks whether their independently consequential responses can constrain and redirect the whole, while permitting voluntary fusion and sacrifice. This criterion is continuous with the philosophical project; it is not a theorem of heat dissipation. Thermodynamic persistence alone does not establish it. Biodiversity as taxonomic richness, complementary metabolism and increasingly capable participants remain related but different claims.
