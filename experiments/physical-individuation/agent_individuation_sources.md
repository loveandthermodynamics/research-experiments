# Individuation: verified prior art and the remaining research gap

Read-only research audit, 21 October 2026. Five complementary primary sources were checked. Their existence rules out the broad claim that nobody has a model of biological individuality. It does not show that a universal physical generator of individuality has been established. In particular, a formal criterion for recognizing an individual and a mechanism that produces one are different achievements.

1. **Queller and Strassmann (2009), “Beyond society: the evolution of organismality.”** Their original framework treats organismality through two axes: actual cooperation among parts and actual conflict among them. It explicitly rejects physical contiguity, indivisibility, clonality and a single-cell bottleneck as universal requirements. It connects symbioses, multicellularity and social colonies to major evolutionary transitions. This is a serious account of what needs explaining, not a derivation of it from microscopic physics. High organismality also does not establish ethical legitimacy: suppressing conflict inside an appropriating collective can remain objectionable. [Primary full text](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2781869/), DOI 10.1098/rstb.2009.0095.

2. **Godfrey-Smith (2009), *Darwinian Populations and Natural Selection*, chapter 4, “Reproduction and Individuality.”** This explicitly asks when a growing and splitting herd reproduces as a collective, rather than only its members reproducing. It separates physiological individuals from genetic individuals and analyzes ramets, colonies, symbioses and chimeras. Reproduction has graded, multidimensional cases with different evolutionary consequences. Spatial integrity therefore cannot settle reproductive individuality. This is an original conceptual framework, not a complete chemical model of a transition. [Author-hosted chapter](https://www.petergodfreysmith.com/Dpops_PGS_Sample_Chapter_4.pdf), [publisher](https://academic.oup.com/book/4808).

3. **Krakauer, Bertschinger, Olbrich, Flack and Ay (2020), “The information theory of individuality.”** This supplies explicit information-theoretic and graphical-model measures of temporal integrity: how candidate aggregates carry information from their past into their future, with different dependence on the environment. It distinguishes organismal, colonial and driven forms and explicitly targets molecular through cultural scales, including distributed entities without anatomical walls. This addresses recognition and measurement. Temporal predictive integrity alone does not establish repair, reproduction, agency or moral standing. [Primary article](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s12064-020-00313-7), DOI 10.1007/s12064-020-00313-7.

4. **Montévil and Mossio (2015), “Biological organisation as closure of constraints.”** This formally distinguishes ongoing thermodynamic processes from relatively conserved constraints that channel those processes. An organized system has a network of constraints that contribute to maintaining one another. Organizational closure is compatible with thermodynamic openness. This directly sharpens organismal integrity beyond a persistent outline or cohesion. The authors explicitly present precise general features for subsequent modeling, rather than a model capturing the complexity of real organisms. [Author-hosted full text](https://montevil.org/publications/articles/2015-mm-organisation-closure-constraints/), DOI 10.1016/j.jtbi.2015.02.029.

5. **Palacios, Razi, Parr, Kirchhoff and Friston (2020), “On Markov blankets and hierarchical self-organisation.”** This is a direct numerical predecessor: nested blankets self-assemble under variational free-energy dynamics with suitable generative priors specifying participation in larger blankets. The authors explicitly disclaim emergence for arbitrary coupled dynamics and identify the origin of the priors as a further evolutionary question. Their free energy is an informational bound on surprise. Thus nested self-organization models already exist; conditional self-assembly does not itself derive those priors or provide the finite-resource thermodynamic and heredity tests of our present project. [Primary author manuscript](https://discovery.ucl.ac.uk/id/eprint/10089189/1/Friston_1-s2.0-S0022519319304588-main.pdf), DOI 10.1016/j.jtbi.2019.110089.

For our experiments, these dimensions should be measured separately. An aggregation has contact, spatial or statistical cohesion. A Darwinian unit has relevant heritable variation and differential reproduction at the proposed level. A self-maintaining organism causally maintains its own functional conditions; it need not itself be fertile. A collective agent additionally requires evidence that joint information processing and action produce persistent, revisable collective dispositions. These are overlapping distinctions, not four automatic stages or a universally agreed definition. A grain and a heap can have genuine physical cohesion without that cohesion demonstrating an organism's active maintenance. Borderline cases do not make every distinction arbitrary.

A Markov blanket is not sufficient. Conditional independence of internal and external variables given a boundary is a statistical property; an ordinary static causal chain can satisfy it without repairing or reproducing anything. Interventions and dynamical mechanisms must establish the stronger claims. Related primary mathematical criticism shows that important free-energy-principle results require assumptions beyond the bare existence of a blanket; it does not refute every version of that research program. [Biehl, Pollock and Kanai (2021)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2001.06408).

The defensible ambition is to demonstrate emergence, maintenance, ancestry and repeated reproduction of collective units from explicit local, thermodynamically paid dynamics, with no cluster identity or reproduction score in the rates. This would be a conditional mechanism to compare with existing work, not proof that physical law universally maximizes individuality or biodiversity. Symbolic and cultural individuality adds representation, role persistence and institutional correction; it is a further modeling challenge, not an area with no prior formal work. Chemical tests should not require Hestia's social prediction or phenomenal consciousness.
