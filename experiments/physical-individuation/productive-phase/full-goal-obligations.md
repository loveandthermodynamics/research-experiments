# Full-goal proof obligations

21 October 2026, implementation/preflight stage. The joined generator supplies copying coupled to two productive transformations, one Hamiltonian and paid reversible chemistry. It has not yet demonstrated dense finite units, a specialization advantage, aging or participant agency. The full goal remains open. The following are dependencies, not parallel authorizations for more experiments.

The current local exports were rechecked: Genesis (private source unavailable) links biodiversity to sustained metabolic pathways; Thermodynamics of Evil (private source unavailable) appeals to future cooperative capacity and accumulated information. Their universal physical conclusions are obligations to correct or establish, not premises already proved. The Meaning of Life (private source unavailable) explicitly joins individual flourishing with collective contribution and rejects suppressing either. This grounds the participant question without making its answer a thermodynamic theorem. These are current exports, not verified original drafts; no separate Group Mind source was located.

| Obligation | Current limit or counterexample | Evidence still required |
|---|---|---|
| **Generated useful differentiation** | Mutation can synthesize an alternative supplied capability; a new label or thermal arrival is insufficient. The catalyst alphabet itself is designed. | Complete the frozen specialist/generalist/ablation comparison. Trace paid founder descendants separately from thermal births; test consequential contribution at matched resources. Appearance, ancestry amplification and aggregate benefit remain separate outcomes. |
| **Biodiversity beyond labels** | [Exact phenotype relabeling](generalist-comparison.md) preserves every path under one genotype; even half-strength generalists can match the ideal specialist output. Complementary functions do not require separate taxa. | Any specialization advantage must survive the full-strength promiscuous control. Later expansion must generate additional heritable functions/interactions and test their maintenance contribution. A fixed two-capability alphabet cannot establish open-ended biodiversity or its universal maximization. |
| **Physical integrity through turnover** | Positive [cycle affinity and renewal currents](balance-and-cost.md) do not establish dense units. One-family equilibrium structure can persist without net paid renewal. The previous phase candidate failed dense formation. | First pass the current finite-domain phase/renewal screens. Then use one prospective local renewal interruption with inventory/work accounting and matched sham/remote support: distinguish restoration from transport or shared-bath activity. No membrane or reproduction requirement should exclude an otherwise causally integrated sterile individual. |
| **Informational aging** | Instance replacement, B-state residence and neutral genealogy contain no demonstrated functional memory. Stationary turnover need not worsen with age. | After a maintained candidate exists, add one physical damage/history state whose retention changes restoration under the same current environment. Compare preserved versus physically erased/reset history and replacement at matched useful function, counting setup, storage, repair and rebuilding. Test whether a lifetime cost advantage actually results. |
| **Recursive individuation** | A cluster, copying subtree or two-level description is not a new higher individual. Relabeling units does not produce another causal level. | Demonstrate one additional level: interacting maintained units form a pattern whose interventions predictably alter mutual restoration while retaining consequential lower-level responses. Test persistence through constituent turnover; assess collective heredity separately. Boundaries may be approximate or overlapping, not necessarily a partition tree. |
| **Least action and thermodynamics** | Local detailed balance constrains a supplied generator. Hamiltonian stationary action, stochastic path cost, entropy production and lifetime work are different quantities. Generalists and dilute dissipative cycles defeat universal diversity/complexity inferences. | Derive any variational claim from this actual generator and state its domain. Compare cumulative physical costs only at fixed useful function. No universal least-action-to-biodiversity theorem survives without additional restrictive premises; cumulative dissipation alone cannot select the desired morality. |
| **Unitology / Resident Evil participant test** | A durable, efficient whole can erase its constituents' interests and capacities. Scalar persistence, fitness, complexity, exergy or entropy throughput cannot independently certify moral legitimacy; putting moral weights into fitness does not derive those weights. | In a later agent-level causal test, vary constituents' consequential needs/options while holding the whole's task/resources comparable. Their responses must be able to constrain and redirect the whole, with development and dependent interests counted. Voluntary fusion, dependency and sacrifice remain possible; permanent molecular or personal separation is not the criterion. This is an explicit normative commitment, not something already measured in chemical templates. |

The immediate next action is the already bounded joined study after independent physics/readout review. Report its phase/renewal claim and near-cluster specialization contrast separately, including failures. A pass would warrant one local integrity experiment; a failure would constrain this grammar rather than authorize a rescue scan. Memory, higher-level inheritance and participant agency depend on those results and need their own physical or cognitive mechanisms. Hestia's moral controls remain load-bearing architectural commitments; none is replaced by a single thermodynamic fitness score.
