# Independent balance and design review

The reviewed `balance-and-cost.md` and `model-spec.md` pass the mathematical design review. This is a review of the specified finite physical generator, before implementation or a spatial study. It does not certify an observed population, phase unit or lineage. The accompanying `balance-independent-certificate.json` records the reviewed source hashes, exact rational local cycle, symbolic balance algebra and high-precision numerical bounds. `verify_balance_independent.py` imports no implementation of the productive model and runs no trajectories.

The two layers conserve working-material carriers A+B and template-precursor units D+X+Y. Every paid birth converts an actual D target and transforms its co-located working material; the parent occupies a different neighboring cell. Each oriented parent/target channel and its reverse must retain the same mutation weight. No factor one half belongs in the explicit same-type, distinct-site reverse. Multiple catalytic parents are parallel channels. A reverse may consume a chemically matching offspring made by another historical parent; ancestry cannot prohibit it. Current helper identity on both directions and historical copying parent on births remain different observations.

The exact identities are

`Delta B=C_X-C_Y+C_0`, `Delta N=C_X+C_Y+I-L`, and `W=mu*(C_X+C_Y)`.

At stationarity they give `J_X=(Lbar-Ibar-J_0)/2` and `J_Y=(Lbar-Ibar+J_0)/2`. Sustained current in both transformations requires `Lbar-Ibar>|J_0|`; it does not establish different taxa, dense material or spatial localization. With common loss mobility delta and `E_r>=max(0,g_r)`, deletion is downhill with rate exactly delta, and the stated unsaturated Boltzmann thermal-birth expression is valid. Outside this energy condition it must be replaced by the actual saturated birth law and a valid loss bound.

For the displayed opposed-binding example, define `c0=2*exp(-6)` and `cB=exp(-8.5)+exp(-3.5)`. Since cB exceeds c0, either whole-family ablation obeys

`E[N] <= [kappa*M/delta+c0*K+(cB-c0)*M]/(1+c0)`.

For K=256, M=77 and delta=.02, its upper bound is **3.21233370883300** templates at kappa=0, or **3.97853526130111** at kappa=.0002. These are expectation bounds, not pathwise ceilings. The finite-time correction `(M+E[N_initial])/[delta*(1+c0)*T]` is valid and retains the effects of initial founders and stored A/B material. The general-affinity positive-part qualification is also correct. Extra thermal sources or material-conversion bypasses would change the bound.

The exact four-step cycle can use a target A/D with two unchanged neighboring helpers X and Y. The certificate independently computes full rational lattice energies for X-copy, offspring loss, Y-copy and offspring loss. Their energy changes are `57/10, -17/2, 44/5, -6`, summing to zero; chemical work is24 at mu12 and the summed log rate ratio is24 at theta1. The physical arrays return exactly. More generally the cycle affinity is `2*mu/theta`.

With both activities enabled, positive finite thermal birth/loss rates, connected transport, a nontrivial working-material inventory and two distinct helper neighbors, the physical occupancy chain is irreducible. Positive stationary entropy production follows from the nonzero cycle affinity. The corrected note properly uses probability fluxes `F=pi(s)q(s,s')`, rather than raw rates, in the nonnegative paired-flux formula. At kappa=0, material balance gives `J_X=J_Y`; stationarity gives `sigma=P/theta` and `P=2*mu*J_X`, so mu>0 implies both mean currents are strictly positive. They may nevertheless be arbitrarily rare. Removing thermal appearance can create an all-D absorbing state and invalidates this particular irreducible-NESS argument. Instance-ID counters and the event journal are observational extensions, excluded from the finite thermodynamic state.

With one activity retained and kappa=0, all work is a state difference: the effective equilibrium Hamiltonian is `H-mu*B` for X-only or `H+mu*B` for Y-only. Stationary net work then vanishes even though thermal templates and structure may remain. Positive kappa reopens a bounded bypass. At positive drive with both activities, the maintenance-power identity counts actual births and losses; more maintained templates can require more work. It supplies no efficiency optimum or moral conclusion.

The local copying costs and residence accounting also pass. X's maximum forward cost is10.5 in the example, while Y's is13.6; mu12 therefore does not saturate every Y event. The B-conversion hazard cap is kappa+4k for equal copy mobilities because D-target forward destruction and occupied-target reverse destruction are mutually exclusive. Regional balances must retain material transport across their boundary. Carrier B-spell duration is a chemical-state residence measure, not molecular replacement or continuity of a droplet.

The generalist comparison passes the separate mathematical review and its certificate rerun. Exact genotype/phenotype relabeling preserves the original path law. The half/half homogeneous generalist has the stated symmetric fixed point, currents and local stability, but is not claimed to have identical stochastic paths. A full-strength promiscuous parent still obeys the same4k instantaneous neighbor cap because A/B targets are mutually exclusive; that cap does not impose a half-rate tradeoff. The localization/allocation calculation is a relaxed fixed-opportunity capacity comparison, not a self-consistent attractor or a uniform impossibility bound. A bypass copier changes the stoichiometric assumptions. None of these alternatives can be excluded by merely omitting them from the reaction alphabet.

No required mathematical correction remains in the reviewed versions. Actual implementation still needs independent local energy/rate/reverse and ledger tests. Dense formation, useful population above thermal sources, causal local renewal, size selection and lineage continuity remain empirical requirements in the joined model; the conserved-enzyme study and homogeneous proof do not establish them.
