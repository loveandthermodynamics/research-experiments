# Renewal occurs. Does a new individual form?

This joined model combines fuel-paid copying, complementary productive roles and attractive material interactions. It tests whether reciprocal activity forms dense units and whether specialists associate with them more strongly than a capable generalist comparator. Those are prospective empirical claims within the supplied chemistry.

B denotes the model’s sticky working material; the templates carrying the productive roles are a separate quantity.

## Both declared claims fail

All **64** histories are retained. B-mass fractions in clusters of at least nine sites in the specialist arms are **20.29%** and **25.85%** at the two sizes, below the criterion requiring a lower confidence bound above **50%**. Specialist-minus-generalist association is **−0.11782** and **−0.09558**, with both intervals entirely below zero. The specialist-association prediction is reversed, rather than merely inconclusive.

Paid reciprocal renewal and genealogical succession still occur. They are useful observations, but do not turn either failed claim into success. Generalists use more fuel as well as supporting more associated templates, so these results do not establish universal generalist superiority or a matched-function efficiency ranking.

## A stronger counterexample to an inference

The [generalist comparison](generalist-comparison.md), P34, shows that complementary functions need not require separate taxa. A one-genotype/two-phenotype interpretation can preserve the complete physical path law, and the specified generalist can match the ideal specialist fixed point and power. This rejects the necessity inference in that model class. It leaves open physically justified constraints that a different model would have to state and test; a penalty invented to make generalists lose would not supply one.
