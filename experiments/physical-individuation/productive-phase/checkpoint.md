# Reciprocal renewal succeeds; dense formation and specialization advantage fail

21 October 2026. Research checkpoint for the Entropy Paradox and Hestia. This joins productive copying and molecular phase interactions in one local stochastic model. It is progress on a prerequisite, not completion of biodiversity, individuation or the Unitology defense.

## One physical chemistry

Each site has a material slot S/A/B and a template slot D/X/Y. A and B are two states of a conserved material carrier; D is template precursor. Both productive transformations make a template and consume counted fuel: X activity converts A to B, while Y activity returns B to A. Fixed copying error can produce the other template phenotype. Every copying, thermal birth/loss and transport channel has its physical reverse. A single Hamiltonian includes B attraction and phenotype-dependent molecular binding. No cluster, group, lineage, diversity or morality readout enters a natural rate.

The comparison begins with dispersed A and X, with no B or Y, at equal resource densities in periodic side-16 and side-32 domains. The four arms are specialists, full-strength promiscuous catalysts, X activity alone and Y activity alone. All retain the same physical alphabet, energies, mutation, thermal template sources, initial arrays and proposal-clock bound. An activity blockade is not deletion of a phenotype. There is no passive A/B bypass in this study. Ideal homogeneous fuel/waste chemostats are supplied premises whose chemical work is counted; constructing the apparatus and preparing the initial molecules is outside the model.

The prospective protocol fixes eight seeds 730100-730107, time 400, samples every five units, early window 100-200 and late window 300-400. Each seed contributes one window mean, not 21 independent observations. All 64 histories remain. No outcome pilot, extra seeds, horizon extension or rescue scan followed.

## What reciprocal renewal establishes

The finite-event balances are exact:

    Delta B = C_X - C_Y + C_0
    Delta N = C_X + C_Y + I - L
    W = mu*(C_X + C_Y) = mu*(Delta N - I + L).

Here C denotes signed forward-minus-reverse copies, C_0 every non-copy A/B conversion, I non-copy births and L passive template losses. At stationary mean, both productive currents are positive exactly when loss minus non-copy supply exceeds the absolute bypass current. This condition concerns transformations, not separate taxa.

With one activity and no A/B bypass, work is a state gradient: +mu*Delta B or -mu*Delta B. The resulting physical chain has an effective equilibrium Hamiltonian. It may make a large static B aggregate while lacking a stationary net paid renewal current. With both activities, a four-step physical cycle returns the occupancies to their initial state while consuming two fuel units; its affinity is 2*mu/theta = 24. Under the stated irreducibility and positive reverse-rate premises, the stationary probability-flux entropy production is positive. This exact theorem does not guarantee a large current, dense unit or lasting ancestry.

The full specialist late X and Y currents have positive lower confidence bounds at both sizes, and its population exceeds each single-activity control. Its exact whole-horizon template density also exceeds the conservative finite-horizon **expectation** bound of 0.05385 for either activity alone. That analytical bound is not a pathwise ceiling on instantaneous counts; Monte Carlo evidence is not an exact proof of an unknown ensemble expectation.

All original X instances disappear in all 16 specialist histories. Paid copying by descendants of those founders nevertheless continues after clearance, and their descendants remain at time 400 in all 16. A qualifying paid Y mutant appears by time 20 in 15/16; its copying subtree reaches four simultaneously living members in 11/16 and remains at the horizon in 9/16. Those subtree counts include both phenotypes after later mutations. Thermal arrivals never resurrect extinct ancestry. This is genuine paid genealogical succession within the supplied chemistry, not organizational memory, phenotype-specific selection, informational aging or a persistent individual.

## What the generalist result changes

Two activities can be necessary even when distinct specialist types are not. The supplied physical model has no genome: exactly the same trajectories can be relabeled as two inherited phenotypes of one genotype. This preserves all rates, costs and ancestry. Chemical labels cannot decide between those interpretations.

A separate homogeneous calculation already shows that even half-strength generalists can match the specialist fixed point, net renewal and chemical power. In the spatial model, a promiscuous parent can have both activities at full mobility without violating the existing per-parent hazard cap: only the substrate-compatible family is eligible at a target. A real synthesis or switching constraint would have to be specified physically. Neither detailed balance nor an observer's preference for biodiversity provides it.

The prospective full-promiscuity control strengthens that objection in this regime. It supports greater near-cluster occupancy and higher total population. It also spends more fuel. The study has not matched useful function, reliability or complete construction costs, so it establishes neither superior generalist efficiency nor a specialist efficiency advantage. It does not compare all possible architectures. A copier bypassing A/B cycling remains unimplemented; resistance to exploitation cannot be claimed from its omission.

## The next connection

The next task is a conditional physical tradeoff, not another scan of attractive parameters. Identify an actual finite catalytic capacity, incompatible local operating conditions, or a structural switching/repair mechanism. Derive a bound on the best admissible adaptive generalist at fixed resources and useful renewal; then ask whether differentiated organization can cross that bound. A cost cannot be inserted merely to obtain the desired ranking. Developmental differentiation and voluntary fusion remain legitimate alternatives to separate species.

Dense shape and metabolic renewal still need a causal connection in the same organization. A new physical mechanism must explain how local renewal preserves the selective structure that returns support to that renewal. A later local interruption with matched sham/remote support would test this only after a maintained candidate exists. Current spatial association is insufficient. Damage, stored regulatory history, replacement and repair costs require physical states; neutral tags and carrier residence times cannot stand in for informational aging.

Ordinary least action has not supplied a universal law selecting maximal biodiversity. A supplied stochastic generator admits a path action, but that does not select its reaction grammar or equate likelihood, entropy production and lifetime cost. The defensible direction is conditional: under demonstrable capacity, environmental and maintenance constraints, differentiated arrangements may outperform specified alternatives. Recursive individuation and open-ended innovation remain further obligations.

The Unitology and Resident Evil challenge remains separate and consequential: a durable or efficient whole can suppress the interests and effective responses of its constituents. Chemical performance neither establishes moral legitimacy nor proves that fusion is wrong. Hestia's participant-oriented moral controls and primacy of social prediction remain architectural commitments; this study does not replace them with a scalar thermodynamic fitness rule.
