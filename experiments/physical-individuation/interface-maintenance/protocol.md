# Reciprocal protection through material turnover

Exploratory implementation protocol, 21 October 2026. The full thread objective remains biodiversity, individuation and a defensible answer to Unitology. This experiment tests a missing physical connection within that objective, not a replacement definition of success.

## Primary physical question

Can dispersed catalytic templates produce a spatially organized, material protective structure through paid local reactions, while that structure helps preserve and renew the templates that produce it?

The model uses material states D, R0, R1 and L on one lattice, a finite fuel/waste layer, local reversible conversion, diffusion, binding and passive turnover. Chemical capacities, interactions and a small founder preparation are supplied premises. No group, shape, lineage or diversity score enters an event rate. Protection changes paired kinetic barriers, not thermodynamic affinities. Energy stored in binding, templates, L and fuel is counted.

## Comparisons fixed before pilot results

Use a two-by-two factorial of L production enabled/disabled and L protection enabled/disabled. All four arms share the Hamiltonian, initial material/fuel preparation, and all unrelated rate parameters. An additional paired copying-channel ablation diagnoses maintenance by regeneration rather than protected survival alone. If protection also changes transport, record transport and passive barriers separately in the specification.

Start the formation assay with dispersed R founders and zero L. Report all trajectories, including failed formation. Do not use a selected successful assembly to estimate formation probability. A later prepared-state or perturbation assay can diagnose reciprocal maintenance conditional on formation, with its preparation and resource changes recorded separately.

Pilot seeds and parameter scans select a feasible kinetic regime; their outcomes remain exploratory. Before a larger ensemble, retain the chosen parameters, seeds, endpoints and comparison rules in a frozen protocol record. Do not silently discard adverse regimes. Do not claim that one favorable regime proves a universal law.

## Readouts

- Time integrals and final values of each template type, L, active contacts and spatial concentrations.
- Count and lifetime of locally connected material assemblies as observations only.
- Formation and loss of productive R-L contacts and L-L organization, including persistence through replacement of founder material.
- Founder-instance retention, paid new instances, interface production and renewal. Tags prove material turnover or ancestry, not functional memory.
- Every fuel conversion, stored-energy change and heat exchange, with independent event replay and conservation checks.
- Continuing productive capacity versus net fuel use and heat over the same horizon. Do not call improved survival alone lower cost. Heat is environmental entropy flow times temperature, not the full ensemble entropy production.

Neutral R0/R1 labels do not establish biodiversity or regulatory information. Functional differentiation requires a demonstrated response or productive difference. Aging requires history-dependent deterioration or recovery of capacity under fixed local rules; ordinary steady turnover alone does not establish senescence.

## Interpretation gates

Production that renews a local protective material and protection that sustains the producing activity support reciprocal material maintenance. The conclusion is stronger if protected founders are replaced by paid descendants while the organization persists. Sticky material or a shell supplied at initialization is insufficient evidence of autonomous formation.

Distinguish a protective local field, a selectively retaining interface, an enclosing membrane, a self-maintained physiological individual and a recursively individuating collective. Each requires its corresponding observation. A successful field must not be promoted to a membrane or an organism merely by terminology.

Report separate outcomes for formation, causal maintenance, turnover, differentiated-response persistence and resource economy. Lower cumulative irreversible loss requires an appropriate entropy/free-energy accounting, beyond event heat. Lower net fuel expenditure per comparable continuing function is a narrower claim.

## Remaining goal requirements

After this missing maintenance loop, the original goal still requires a physical account of variable collective inheritance and repeated individuation, retained functional differentiation under shocks, and the proposed relation to a variational principle. The ethical Unitology defense additionally needs independently answerable participants and a causal reason their flourishing affects collective conduct. Persistence or energetic efficiency alone cannot establish moral legitimacy. Keep those requirements open rather than substituting the present experiment for them.
