# What a physical individuation argument can prove

Read-only audit of the previous experiments; this new note does not change their sources, results, or frozen bundles. The strongest defensible claim is conditional: differentiated responses plus physically paid reciprocal regeneration can maintain more available free energy from the same finite inventory. A theorem of that kind can explain an advantage of some organizations. Neither a dissipation scalar nor cumulative resource cost can, by itself, establish a universal biodiversity optimum or the moral legitimacy of an organization.

## A finite-fuel theorem for actual maintained availability

Consider two labeled sites, each either active or inactive. An active site stores energy epsilon; there are K>=1 fuel packets, each with energy gap e>epsilon, and a bath with thermal energy theta. Initially both sites are active and all packets are fuel. The two comparisons have the same state space, energy levels, initial state, and equilibrium reference.

In the independent-response case, each active site fails at rate lambda. In the common-response case, a rate-lambda event disables all currently active sites. Each site's marginal failure hazard is therefore identical. In either case, one active site can restore an inactive partner at rate mu f, consuming one of the remaining f fuel packets. No repair is possible after both sites become inactive in the negligible-reverse limit.

Complete every edge reversibly. Independent thermal activation has rate lambda exp(-epsilon/theta). The reverse of a common failure into a mask with n active sites has rate lambda exp(-n epsilon/theta). Reverse repair has rate mu w exp[-(e-epsilon)/theta], where w is the waste count. These paired rates obey detailed balance for

\[
\pi(a,f)\propto {K\choose f}\exp[-(\epsilon n(a)+ef)/\theta].
\]

The collective common-failure edge is a supplied coarse physical channel, not a derivation from elementary spatial chemistry. The active subsystem has the SAME marginal reference in both comparisons. Its actual availability is

\[
B_A(t)=\epsilon E[n(t)]-\theta H(p_A(t))
       +2\theta\log(1+e^{-\epsilon/\theta}),
\]

where H is Shannon entropy in nats. This is theta times relative entropy to the Gibbs reference, not an assigned diversity or welfare score.

First take the negligible-reverse limit. The common-response mean activity is m_C(t)=2 exp(-lambda t). Let r(t) be expected successful repair flux in the independent case. Then

\[
\dot m_I=-\lambda m_I+r(t),\qquad
\Delta A_0(H)\equiv\int_0^H(m_I-m_C)dt
=\frac1\lambda\int_0^H[1-e^{-\lambda(H-s)}]r(s)ds>0.
\]

The inequality is strict for every H>0 when mu>0 and at least one packet is available: independent damage can leave a repairer, and actual repair contributes the additional activity. Fuel use never exceeds K. With mu=0 the difference is exactly zero.

This survives finite temperature. Bound the additional reverse-event exit rates by

\[
L_I=2\lambda e^{-\epsilon/\theta}
       +2\mu K e^{-(e-\epsilon)/\theta},
\]
\[
L_C=\lambda(2e^{-\epsilon/\theta}+e^{-2\epsilon/\theta})
       +2\mu K e^{-(e-\epsilon)/\theta}.
\]

Couple each finite-temperature chain to its negligible-reverse chain until their first extra reverse event. The probability of separation before t is at most L t, so their mean active counts differ by at most 2 L t. Also 0<=H(p_A)<=log 4. Consequently

\[
\boxed{\int_0^H(B_I-B_C)dt
\geq \epsilon[\Delta A_0(H)-H^2(L_I+L_C)]
      -2\theta H\log2.}
\]

Whenever the right-hand side is positive, independent responses and paid regeneration provably maintain more active-subsystem availability over that horizon. For any fixed positive H, it is positive for sufficiently small positive theta. Total closed-system availability still decreases; material and fuel availability and their correlations must all be counted.

An explicit instance needs no new simulation. For K=1 and lambda=mu=1, the successful repair flux is r(s)=2s exp(-2s), hence

\[
\Delta A_0(H)=\tfrac12-2e^{-H}+(\tfrac32+H)e^{-2H}.
\]

Choose epsilon=1, e=2, theta=.05, H=4. Then Delta A_0=.4652137667, the reverse-event correction is below .00000026383, and the entropy correction is .2772588722. The rigorous lower bound on integrated availability advantage is **.1879546306 energy-times-time units**. This is a theorem about a supplied kinetic architecture, not a result of the new spatial pilot. Energy-times-time units alone do not turn this functional into Hamiltonian action.

## Exact obstructions to universality

**Diversity without regeneration need not increase availability.** With no repair and p=exp(-lambda t), the negligible-reverse independent distribution has mask entropy 2h(p), whereas the common-response distribution has entropy h(p). Both have mean activity 2p. Thus common response has greater active availability by theta h(p), up to exponentially small finite-temperature reverse corrections. At sufficiently small positive theta this remains a strict counterexample: correlations themselves store availability. The regeneration theorem succeeds because restored energetic capacity can exceed that entropy difference.

**More species is not more free energy by definition.** For two isoenergetic ideal-mixture species at fixed total amount N, an equal mixture has free energy lower than the pure state by theta N log2. Functional differentiation, complementary production, response diversity, and future renewal must establish any advantage. A count of labels cannot supply it.

**Neither less nor more dissipation is sufficient.** Without a maintained-function constraint, inactivity can minimize ongoing dissipation. Conversely, a futile fuel-consuming cycle can increase dissipation without improving renewal. Even at matched output, different architecture-specific setup and repair costs can favor centralization, short-lived replacement, or modular longevity. The earlier renewal inequalities identify the conditions; they do not imply that every hierarchy is cheaper.

**Entropy production is not the complete physical path action.** For a continuous-time Markov trajectory, minus log path probability contains both jump-rate terms and the integrated escape rate. Entropy production determines a forward/reverse path-probability ratio under local detailed balance, not the entire path probability. Multiplying both directions of an edge by the same barrier factor preserves its affinity while changing organization kinetics and lifetimes. The interface experiment deliberately uses this freedom. A least-dissipation principle cannot replace the missing kinetic argument.

**Available energy does not determine participant agency.** A transition permission or symmetric barrier can change counterfactual access and effective ability to leave, communicate, or revise a commitment without changing the state's energy levels. Along a history where that option is unused, energetic accounts can be identical. Preserving all microstate information in an archive likewise does not establish that the original participants remain able to pursue or revise their projects. These are causal and normative questions beyond the scalar energy account.

The ethical Unitology critique can therefore remain coherent even against an efficient, information-preserving adversary: appropriating ongoing participants for an imposed convergence can violate their interests and agency. Their standing cannot depend solely on whether they increase the collective's thermodynamic performance. Informed voluntary sacrifice, development, and fusion remain possible; the critique need not freeze existing selves or forbid every higher-level integration. Dependent participants and outsiders also matter when they cannot reciprocally increase output. Physics can test material feasibility and ecological consequences; intrinsic participant value supplies the ethical reason for caring about those consequences.

## Explicit next propositions and a recursive proof strategy

1. **Self-produced protection, not protected survival alone.** In the new factorial experiment, establish that paid R production creates L, L causally changes paired damage/transport barriers, and continued R copying replenishes the protected capacity. Preserve founders, descendant instances, interface turnover, and failed trajectories as separate observations. The production/protection interaction and copying ablation should diagnose the closed maintenance loop.

2. **Differentiated response, not neutral labels.** Give R0 and R1 demonstrably different responses to matched environmental disturbances; retain the same per-capita marginal exposure and account for common events. Test whether some class remains able to rebuild others, including founder dilution, insufficient fuel, excessive coupling, and common-mode counterexamples. The theorem above supplies a small exact benchmark for the intended mechanism.

3. **A localized restoring regime, not a concentration branch.** Demonstrate an autonomously formed, spatially localized assembly with no assigned container boundary; perturb it while conserving and recording material and preparation work. Show recovery through new instances after founder loss. Distinguish a protective field, selective interface, enclosing boundary, and reproducing unit.

4. **Variable collective inheritance.** Produce more than one descendant organization through local physical copying/partitioning events. A new organization should carry a causally transmissible variation that changes its own maintenance or reproduction. Spatial recurrence alone is insufficient. Compare descendants under the same resources and disturbances without adding a group fitness reward.

5. **An extension lemma for a second level.** Starting with such level-one units, use the SAME local reaction grammar to generate contacts or interfaces among them. Show that a new maintained organization appears while constituent units retain observable renewal. Do not introduce a privileged level-two label or a fresh free interface. Derive the costs, inherited variation, and response dependence of this higher unit from the microscopic dynamics.

6. **Controlled coarse-graining and selection.** If internal relaxation is faster than inter-unit events, derive effective transition rates by first-passage analysis. Verify approximate Markov closure or quantify its memory; retain hidden dissipation and interaction energy. Couple any resource saving to actual physical descendant production under finite stocks or explicit replenishment. A cheaper structure need not reproduce or invade unless that causal connection exists.

An induction could then establish repeated individuation over a stated range: each level produces viable, variable descendants, a same-grammar extension preserves its constituent renewal, and its repair advantage exceeds its setup, regulatory, transport, and correlated-failure costs. Biodiversity additionally requires maintained response differentiation or coexistence, not only growth of one successful hierarchy. Finite material imposes a depth bound: nonoverlapping branching groups containing at least b retained constituents per level have depth at most log_b N. The plausible theorem is recursive organization under specified conditions and resource limits, not inevitable unlimited hierarchy or a universal maximum of biodiversity.
