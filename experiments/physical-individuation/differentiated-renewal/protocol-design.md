## Supplied physics and pairing

Use the previously selected driven chemistry at copying coefficient .028, 8 by 8 periodic sites, eight dispersed R0 founders, zero L, 58 fuel units, and the same supplied reservoir exchange. The copying-error kernel is fixed at [[.95,.05],[.05,.95]] in every arm for the entire trajectory. No mutation switch follows appearance. The gate has physical states -1,+1, equal zero energy, symmetric transition rate kappa and a stationary seeded initial sign. Its independent clock and initial sign are shared across all paired arms of a setting. Gate flips have zero energy change, work and heat; this does not imply zero total entropy production in the driven process. Material and reservoir events remain stochastic and may diverge across arms.

For R0, passive mobility is multiplied by 1+epsilon*gate. In the opposed arm, R1 has 1-epsilon*gate; in the aligned arm, R1 has the same factor as R0. Multiply both birth and loss directions of each passive pair. All wells, mutation costs and marginal stationary mean passive multipliers match. Responses are supplied chemical capabilities, not newly invented traits. The experiment asks whether realized differentiation contributes to regeneration under these rules.

Cross opposed/aligned with paid L production enabled/disabled, keeping protective effects and thermal L channels present. Disable both directions of the paid production channel consistently. Every cell starts from the same physical founder/fuel state and runs to time 100. This end-to-end assay includes formation and maintenance; it does not isolate postformation maintenance. Keep every trajectory, including failed formation, absent mutants and demographic loss.

## Bounded pilot and fresh confirmation

The pilot has four settings: epsilon in {.5,.8}, crossed with kappa in {.025,.1}; eight fixed seeds 1050001 through 1050008; four arms per setting; 128 full trajectories. These finite amplitudes are not asserted to lie in the small-forcing proof's uncomputed validity interval. Select the setting maximizing the minimum pilot mean/standard-error ratio of the two primary contrasts below. Break an exact tie by smaller epsilon, then smaller kappa. The pilot is exploratory and makes no confirmatory claim.

Freeze a single confirmation using that setting, with 128 fresh seeds 1060001 through 1060128 and four arms per seed, 512 trajectories. The seed range, chemistry, horizon, outcomes and interval rule are fixed before pilot outcomes are read. Retain the pilot data digest in the confirmation protocol. Do not pool the pilot, stop early, tune another setting after confirmation, or conduct a rescue scan.

The outcome A is the exact event-integrated number of active templates. The two paired primary contrasts are:

1. A(opposed,production on)-A(aligned,production on).
2. [A(opposed,on)-A(aligned,on)]-[A(opposed,off)-A(aligned,off)].

For each, report the mean, standard error and two-sided t127 95% interval across fresh seed pairs. Also report a fixed 10,000-replicate paired percentile-bootstrap sensitivity using seed 1080001 and common resampled seed indices for both contrasts. It does not replace the declared t criterion. The declared joint claim requires both t lower endpoints above zero. A positive first contrast alone supports a functional response effect in the full supplied chemistry; a positive interaction alone is insufficient and could arise from harm in another arm. Report all four arm means and both contrasts whatever their signs. The primary statistical unit is the seed, not individual events or gate jumps.

Record the first R1 appearance of any origin and separately the first paid R0-catalyzed R1 birth by time 20. Mark that molecule's copying descendants offline, including descendants that mutate back to R0. Unrelated thermal births, later outside-subtree mutants and reused precursor never inherit its mark. No qualifying birth gives zero in the all-seed appearance/establishment/survival outcomes; do not select only successful mutants.

The declared finite-time establishment event is at least four live descendants of that first paid mutant, before subtree extinction or time 100. Report its probability/count, final subtree survival and size, exact integrated subtree R1, first loss and subsequent restoration of R1 within the subtree, and the time with both types present. Expansion over both alleles is not uninterrupted R1 persistence or a rare-type invasion exponent. Positive R1 occupancy can be maintained by recurrent copying errors or thermal immigration. A positive response contrast is a counterfactual functional contribution, not proof that the focal subtree has positive selection in every state.

Count supplied work, heat, fuel, passive births, paid copying, mutation and protective production throughout. Include initial stored energy. Activity-time is not stored negentropy, total entropy production or an efficiency claim. The full goal requires causal organization and further individuation beyond these tests.
