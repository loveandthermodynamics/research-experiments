## A concrete spatial mechanism to investigate next

Local support still leaves a size-selection problem. A finite phase-separated unit maintained through material turnover is a more specific physical target than a contact cluster. [Active-droplet research](https://www.nature.com/articles/nphys3984) supplies examples of fueled growth and division; [thermodynamically consistent condensate models](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2103.02921) connect driving, molecular partitioning and finite-size regulation. These are prior physical models, not results already reproduced in our lattice.

The separate [active-droplet bridge](active-droplet-bridge.md) derives an illustrative three-dimensional radial reduction:

\[
\dot R=A/R-C/R^2-BR.
\]

Supply, curvature and volume-proportional turnover give two positive stationary radii when 4*A^3>27*B*C^2. The smaller is unstable and the larger radially stable. An outward certificate and independent exact-rational review verify one example. This assumes coexisting phases, a thin interface and a supplied flux law. It does not derive nucleation, nonspherical stability, division, the current two-dimensional lattice or a full individual. It identifies a possible physical length scale without a preferred-size reward.
