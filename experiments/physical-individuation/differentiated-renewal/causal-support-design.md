# Prospective fixed-location renewal assay

This is an external kinetic causal assay of the same finite driven chemistry. It is separate from the end-to-end response comparison. It must not turn observer-defined groups or neutral ancestry into autonomous rate feedback, or claim that a blocker apparatus was constructed for free.

Use the response parameters selected by the already bounded appearance/contribution pilot. Freeze them and this assay's source hashes before any of its outcomes. Take 64 fresh seeds 1070001 through 1070064. Each preparation runs the full opposed-response model from dispersed R0 and zero L for 100 time units, with fixed mutation and all original chemical rates. Retain failed and empty preparations.

From each physical endpoint, select once a 2 by 2 periodic square maximizing R count, then L count, with lexicographic tie breaking. This defines a candidate region for an experiment, not a boundary recognized by the chemistry. Choose a disjoint remote square with periodic Manhattan center distance at least 3, minimizing the difference in initial total paid L-production propensity. Break ties by larger distance and then lexicographic coordinates. The square selects helper/catalyst sites; target locations may lie outside it.

Record the near and remote initial forward and reverse production propensities. Let their sums be Hnear and Hremote. If both are positive and the preparation contains templates, set removed fractions qnear=min(1,Hremote/Hnear) and qremote=min(1,Hnear/Hremote). The corresponding retained site factors are 1-q in the selected square, and 1 elsewhere. This matches the initial removed total propensity to min(Hnear,Hremote). It does not match net productive current, barrier strength, future flux or outcome; report these differences. If no eligible pair exists or either propensity is zero, retain the preparation with both lesions set to no-op and an unmatched flag. Do not discard it or replace it with a favorable preparation.

Continue each prepared state for 80 units under six arms: opposed/aligned responses crossed with sham/near/remote lesions. All six preserve the actual material, fuel, instance IDs, next-tag counter and gate state at branching. Reset only elapsed-time and cumulative-ledger counters. Use a new fixed post seed equal to origin+3000000; its independent gate stream is shared across all six arms. Mutation remains fixed throughout. The response-map and fixed-site barrier changes are declared counterfactual interventions, not paid natural evolution. Both directions of each selected production pair receive the same immutable site factor. Energies and per-event work/heat stay unchanged; apparatus construction and measurement costs are outside the model.

The primary observable Y is exact integrated R count in the near square plus its nearest-neighbor shell. Primary paired contrasts across all 64 seeds are sham-minus-near and remote-minus-near in the opposed map. The declared local-support claim requires both two-sided t63 95% intervals to have positive lower bounds. Report a fixed 10,000-replicate paired percentile-bootstrap sensitivity with seed 1080002, without replacing this criterion. The response-map interaction in remote-minus-near and the absolute sham opposed-minus-aligned effect are additional named outcomes; they are not substitutes for failed primary contrasts. No pilot pooling or rescue scan occurs.

Track the copying descendants of R molecules in the near square at the preparation endpoint, globally and in the frozen readout region. Mutation does not break genealogy; unrelated thermal births do not join it. Count inward and outward crossings, original-cohort instance clearance, copying after that clearance, and forward-minus-reverse protective production attributed to cohort helpers using their pre-event tags. These annotations never enter rates. A molecule crossing a coordinate boundary is not dead; local loss and global descendant extinction must remain distinct.

Include full preparation and continuation work, heat, energy and raw histories. Shared supply conditions do not imply equal realized work. Initial blocked propensities are matched; actual production and future propensity exposures may diverge as consequences of the lesions. If sampled hazard-exposure diagnostics are added, label their quadrature explicitly rather than calling them prevented events. A positive effect would establish support from continuing production at fixed nearby locations over this protocol. It would not by itself establish ownership, a self-bounded individual, retained organizational memory, physical aging, reproduction or ethical participant status.
