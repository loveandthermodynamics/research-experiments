# Does differentiation improve renewal—and for whom?

The positive proposal is that different productive activities can sustain one another and open further possibilities for organization. This stage tests narrower response-benefit claims and asks whether a local intervention reveals consequential support.

The two template types in this study differ in vulnerability responses but have the same productive chemistry. This is a test of response diversity, rather than a division of productive roles.

## The failed benefit and the narrower support result

The confirmation fails both declared positive diversity-benefit gates. Mutant descendants and active renewal occur, but their presence does not supply the missing benefit. A later near/sham/remote intervention finds that blocking nearby production reduces local activity. That is evidence of a local supporting relation under the supplied intervention.

The later result does not rescue the earlier diversity claim or identify a self-bounded individual. The blockade is an externally imposed, initially hazard-matched intervention on a fixed region; its strength, future exposure and overlapping neighborhoods matter. The radial size-selection theorem is another conditional construction, not evidence that these lattice histories form, divide or inherit an organism.

## Relation to the larger question

“Unitology” in the October notes is a fictional *Dead Space* reference, used to challenge the idea that a successful successor automatically benefits its incorporated members. The present tests concern chemical renewal and differentiation. They do not test responsibility to people. New collective and member identities remain admissible; their continuing possibilities, care and influence on action would have to be measured in a suitable developmental study.
