The persistence step was checked against [Benaim's Stochastic Persistence, Theorems 4.4 and 6.1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1806.08450). Gate jumps do not change this function, so its generator martingale and jump variance vanish. Compactness supplies the remaining tightness bounds. These are the average-Lyapunov conditions needed for occupation-measure persistence; no claim of uniqueness of the interior law is necessary.

The result requires both types initially positive inside the high region. A source-free concentration model is essential to its invariant-boundary argument. The positive theorem and these limitations should be reported together.
