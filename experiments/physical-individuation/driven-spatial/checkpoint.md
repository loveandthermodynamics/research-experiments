# Driven organization and conditional diversity

Research checkpoint, 21 October 2026. The full biodiversity, individuation and Unitology research goal remains active and unproved. No Hestia runtime, RFC or essay was changed.

## What now fits together

The conjecture has three separable physical links: activity produces constraints that sustain activity; differentiated responses can persist within that renewing organization; a particular organization preserves its causal integrity while its constituents change. This checkpoint strengthens the first two links in distinct supplied models. It leaves the third unresolved and does not claim that the links have all been demonstrated in one system.

This is substantially stronger than merely saying organization creates favorable conditions for differentiation. A conditional invasion calculation now identifies when a rare, differently vulnerable type grows, and an existence proof gives actual two-type persistence for sufficiently weak environmental forcing. The same equations also provide an adverse case. A universal instruction to maximize species count would be incorrect.

## 1. Continuous flow funds ongoing spatial renewal

The new driven lattice extends the frozen reversible interface chemistry with explicit fuel/waste exchange at every site. Reservoir chemical work, stored energy and bath heat are recorded event by event. The supplied reaction grammar contains copying, protective-material production, binding and transport; none depends on an observer's group label, a diversity score or a reward. Initially eight templates are dispersed on an 8 by 8 periodic lattice, with no protective material. Founders, initial fuel and material interactions remain premises.

A bounded pilot selected a copying rate from three declared candidates. The fresh confirmation retained all 64 preparations and branched each into production-by-protection interventions crossed with two transport graphs. All branches share the prepared physical state. The primary outcome is integrated template count over the continuation window, not stored negentropy or a measure of individuality.

## 2. A maintained state can be reached without supplying its protection

A separate deterministic assay tests the previously certified reversible well-mixed maintenance chemistry. All seven founder concentrations start with zero protective material. The full mechanism reaches the high state from .05, .10, .20, .50 and .90; .01 and .03 decay to the low state. Removing protective production, protective function or both leaves all 21 corresponding assays low.

The 28 trajectories include cumulative supplied chemical work and initial material free-energy differences. A separately reconstructed DOP853 calculation agrees with the saved RK45 paths to a maximum state discrepancy of 2.22e-10. This is a finite deterministic founder grid, not a 5/7 formation probability or a proof of the minimum founder threshold. The well-mixed reactor does not establish spatial individuation.

## 3. Differentiation can be self-maintaining, conditionally

The source-free concentration model supplies two classes with opposite responses to an autonomous switching environment. It uses the negligible-reverse, zero-immigration large-copy approximation: absent-type immigration is omitted while fuel remains supplied. They share copying and protection-production chemistry; their susceptibility to passive loss differs. Their joint activity renews finite-lived protective material. No target type proportion or diversity bonus appears.

For a persistent resident, the missing type's exact long-run growth is the difference between resident and invader losses after accounting for their copying rates. Near a stable unforced high state, equal-copying opposite responses give

\[
\lambda_{\mathrm{rare}}=K\varepsilon^2+O(\varepsilon^3),\qquad
\operatorname{sign}K=\operatorname{sign}(D_0-S_0),
\]

where epsilon is forcing amplitude, D0 available precursor and S0 active templates. Protection and resource availability respond with a delay to environmental changes. The covariance between that response and the gate determines whether the missing response can grow.

Independent outward interval arithmetic certifies K between .189215105435 and .189215105460 at inventory .95, and between -.120559965164 and -.120559965144 at inventory 1.5. Thus the same mechanism can favor or oppose rare differentiation. These are coefficients, not finite-amplitude simulation results.

The new small-forcing proof supplies a common invariant high neighborhood, unique contracting resident laws, a uniform response remainder and strictly positive mutual invasion for some sufficiently small nonzero forcing interval in the positive case. The average-Lyapunov criterion then proves occupation-measure persistence with both types initially present in that neighborhood. Continuing copying, protective production and protective turnover remain positive. See [the full proof](small-forcing-proof.md), [independent review](theory-review.md), and [Benaim's persistence framework](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1806.08450).

This is a real local persistence theorem in the declared concentration model. It supplies no numerical permitted forcing amplitude. It omits finite-population extinction and thermal immigration; those changes invalidate its invariant-zero-facet argument. Copying error can supply appearance, but appearance and subsequent persistence under fixed mutation have not been joined by this proof. Nor does two-type coexistence establish unlimited branching: a continuous response trait admits an insensitive competitor unless an additional physical tradeoff is justified. The present driven lattice's two template labels are functionally identical and supply no diversity test.

## The variational connection and the individuality gap

A supplied chemical generator has a large-volume stochastic action. Its nonnegative Lagrangian vanishes on its reaction-rate dynamics. This provides a precise conditional action formulation of maintenance and, conditional on the environmental gate history, differentiated persistence. The generator still supplies the chemistry, barriers, resources and possible alternatives. Several basins can all have zero-action histories. This is a probability cost, distinct from Hamilton's energy-times-time action and from entropy production. See [the stochastic-action bridge](stochastic-action-bridge.md) and its [primary chemical-network source](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1902.08416).

Information-based individuality offers useful continuous, nested measures without requiring a membrane. Such a measure does not itself generate or validate an individual: an inert stored random bit can be perfectly predictive without metabolic regeneration. The proposed individuality test therefore requires physical interventions, turnover and retained predictive organization together. No reliable mutual-information estimate or organizational memory is claimed from the current lattice samples. See [the measurement note](individuation-measure.md) and [Krakauer and colleagues](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s12064-020-00313-7).

The next physical step is to put genuinely different response classes into the same fully accounted driven spatial maintenance process, then distinguish their establishment and functional contribution from thermal immigration and neutral labels. Individuation needs a causal test of locally produced support: compare interruption of the candidate organization's own renewal with a matched remote intervention under the same supply, while measuring recovery through constituent replacement. Candidate-group labels must remain observations and interventions, never rate rewards. Neither a favorable scale exponent nor another level of hierarchy should substitute for this missing causal connection.

For the Unitology adversary, allow the strongest case: fusion could be durable, efficient and even voluntarily chosen. The physical work must identify when eliminating differentiated responses destroys capacities or raises correlated failure. The ethical argument must separately ask whose interests and consequential independence survive integration. No single thermodynamic scalar settles both questions.
