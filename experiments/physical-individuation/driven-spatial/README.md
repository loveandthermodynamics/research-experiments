# Does local renewal sustain more than a shared supply?

This stage moves from well-mixed maintenance toward spatial organization under continuing reservoir supply. Sustained activity is one result; a specific advantage from keeping support local is another.

## Result and limit

The initial spatial confirmation met its declared normal-interval locality rule, while its Student-t sensitivity interval crossed zero. The later, fresh **256-preparation** replication gives a local-minus-mixed mean of **25.01** template-time units and a 95% t interval of **−17.85 to 67.86**. The replication does not establish the proposed locality advantage. It also does not establish that the true effect is zero.

Separate conditional theory supports differentiated persistence in a specified regime. It retains an adverse regime, rather than proving that diversity is always advantageous. A stochastic path-action formulation supplies a probability cost for the given chemistry, distinct from mechanical action or entropy production; it does not itself generate organisms.

## What remains to be shown

A local advantage would still need to produce and maintain a candidate organization, rather than merely improve a supplied system. Further development of members and wholes needs additional comparisons. The historical references to Unitology (*Dead Space*) and *Resident Evil* are adversarial thought experiments about a successor whose success erases those incorporated into it. These physical tests do not disprove fictional doctrines or establish moral legitimacy. See [the positive hypothesis and its possible failures](../../../docs/interpreting-results.md).
